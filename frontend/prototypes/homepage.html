    The idea behind Colyv

    One line. Every creator. One place.

    A kolam is drawn around its dots in one unbroken line. Colyv works the same way. The dots are creators. The line is a brand, connecting to every one of them in a single place, with every step of every deal on one record.

    • Each dot is a creator: their Passport, their prices, their delivery record.
    • The line is the brand's one view of a campaign: applications, matches and deals together.
    • Nothing falls outside the line. Every step of a deal is added to it, in order, and sealed.

            What Colyv never does

            The promises that shape the product

            Four rules built into the service, not slogans.

            Never holds your money

            Brands pay creators directly. Colyv records what was paid and when.

            Never hands out contact details

            Brands see a Passport, not a phone number. Contact opens only after a deal is accepted.

            Never rewrites history

            Deal records are chained and sealed. The database itself refuses to edit or delete them.

            Never calls a number verified when it is not

            Follower counts are the creator's own claim, dated and labelled as such.

            Ask Colyv
            Does Colyv hold or move our money?

            No. Brands pay creators directly, by UPI or bank transfer. Colyv records that a brand marked a payment as sent, and that the creator confirmed it arrived, with dates.

            Is a deal memo a legal contract?

            No. It is a record of the terms both sides explicitly accepted. Colyv shows what was recorded and does not claim legal force.

            Who can see a creator's Passport?

            Anyone with the link, but only after the creator publishes it. Prices are a second, separate choice. Phone numbers and follower counts are never on it.

            What if there is not enough data about someone?

            Colyv says so. Delivery shares appear only after 3 completed deals, fair-rate figures only when at least 5 creators back them, and overdue work is always shown.

            How do I know a deal's record has not been changed?

            Each entry is sealed to the one before it, and the database refuses to edit or delete a sealed entry. A daily checkpoint is time-stamped by two outside authorities, so a rewrite would show.

            What does it cost?

            Pricing is not final. Join the pilot list and you will hear before anything is charged.

            Can I sign in today?

            Sign-in with a phone code opens with the pilot. Until then, join the list and we will invite you.

            Do you check follower counts?

            Not yet. Channel figures are the creator's own claim, dated and labelled that way, and the public Passport does not show them.

            What happens if a payment does not arrive?

            The creator raises a dispute from the payment screen. Both sides add evidence to one neutral timeline and each records how it ended. Colyv never decides who is right.

            Is my data safe, and can I leave?

            Every public endpoint is rate limited, writes are safe to repeat, and you can export everything Colyv holds about you. In-app account deletion and the final consent wording are being completed with legal review.
