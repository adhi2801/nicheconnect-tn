# Prototypes

Design prototypes, kept here for reference. They are single HTML files with sample data and no backend. Open them in a browser.

| File | What it is | Published copy |
|---|---|---|
| `dashboard.html` | Brand and creator dashboard (use the Brand view / Creator view switch). Black and butter yellow on cream: the earlier look, **retired on 28 Oct 2026** in favour of the green direction in `design/screen-plan.md`. Kept for its screens and content, not its colours. | https://claude.ai/artifact/MniAhS5AMPgAhFtZvEDa7F |
| `homepage.html` | Marketing homepage with the 3D temple scene. Heritage burgundy and gold, which still has to be restyled to the one theme. | https://claude.ai/artifact/JDpfAfKdxJ65GPrL55hS4p |
| `fold/` | The homepage's scroll section "The fold is the deal" (`fold.css`, `fold-screens.js`, `fold.js`): a folding phone modelled from the iPhone Duo's published proportions, no maker's logo, played by scroll in six chapters, with the brand and the creator on either half and the deal's seal drawn across the crease from its own fingerprint. A still version for no WebGL, weak devices and reduce motion. | https://claude.ai/artifact/GPHNZuh7aky3z3sYEY6U1i (preview with the section) |

These are not production code. They use fonts from Google Fonts and the homepage loads its 3D scene inline. The real apps are `apps/web` (Next.js) and `apps/mobile` (Flutter).
