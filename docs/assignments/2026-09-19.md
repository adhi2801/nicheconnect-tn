WHERE WE ARE (plain words)
A brand can post a campaign, a creator can find it and apply, the brand can shortlist,
accept or reject with a reason, write a deal memo, send it, and the creator can accept it,
submit proof of the work, and have it approved — or approved automatically after 7 days.
Both sides get notified at every step. Login, profiles, rate limits and error handling are done.
What it still cannot do: record that money was paid, show a brand's payment record, handle
disputes, match creators to campaigns, or send anything by WhatsApp.

(this command plus file ownership). Nothing is broken: 599 tests pass, 16 migrations rebuild
